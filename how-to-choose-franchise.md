# How to Choose an Edutech Franchise

Choosing an edutech franchise requires careful evaluation of both educational quality and business requirements.

## Curriculum

Check whether the curriculum is structured, age-appropriate, practical, and regularly updated.

## Trainer Support

Find out how instructors are trained and whether ongoing academic support is available.

## Equipment

Understand the required computers, robotics kits, sensors, software, and other learning resources.

## Student Projects

Ask whether students receive opportunities to build and test practical projects.

## Costs

Review the complete financial model, including:

- Initial franchise fee
- Infrastructure costs
- Equipment costs
- Training costs
- Marketing expenses
- Renewal fees
- Other recurring charges

## Certification

Certification claims should be clearly documented. Understand who issues certificates and what they represent.

## Existing Partners

Where possible, speak with existing franchise partners to understand their experience with training, support, operations, and student enrollment.

## Final Checklist

A good franchise evaluation should consider educational quality, practical resources, trainer support, transparency, and long-term sustainability.
